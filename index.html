Open computational research

Finding leads for diseases that few people study.

Thousands of rare diseases have no approved treatment. We use public biomedical data, AI, and simulation to generate testable hypotheses about existing drugs, then publish our methods, code, and results openly so that clinicians and labs can follow up.

See the research How we stay honest

Important: Everything on this site is a computational prediction. None of it has been tested in a laboratory or by a regulator, none of it is a treatment or a cure, and none of it is medical advice. Please talk to a qualified clinician about any health decision.

Our approach

A repeatable pipeline from public data to a published, checkable hypothesis.

  1. Choose a disease

    We pick conditions that are rare, neglected, and have open data and an active patient community.

  2. Mine public data

    We combine open drug, gene, and disease databases to score which existing compounds might be relevant.

  3. Stress-test

    We check whether our method re-discovers known drug-disease pairs before trusting any new prediction.

  4. Publish openly

    We release ranked candidates, code, and limitations as preprints and, where possible, in journals.

What we do

  • Publish methods, code, and data sources.
  • Report negative and uncertain results.
  • State every limitation next to every result.
  • Hand promising leads to people who can test them.

What we don't do

  • Claim a cure or recommend a treatment.
  • Run clinical trials or give medical advice.
  • Treat a prediction as proof it works.
  • Ask patients for money or personal health data.
