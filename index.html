Cure A Disease

We write open, plain-language breakdowns of early-stage ideas for diseases that still have no cure: what goes wrong in the body, how an idea might fix it, what is unknown, and what it would take to reach patients.

Please read: every idea on this site is a research concept. None is FDA approved, none has been tested in humans, and none is a treatment or medical advice. Talk to a doctor about any health decision.

Why a nonprofit

Hard diseases are often neglected because the market for them is small, slow, or risky. We are not here to sell anything. Our goal is to make good ideas easier to understand, criticise, and hand to people with the labs and licences to test them.

We would rather say "we don't know yet" than oversell. Each breakdown lists its unknowns right next to its promise.

  • We doExplain biology and research ideas in plain language and show our reasoning.
  • We don'tClaim a cure, give medical advice, run clinical trials, or ask patients for money or health data.
  • StatusEvery idea is at concept stage. "Not FDA approved" and "not tested in humans" are on every page for a reason.
  • AccountsFree. An account lets you save diseases to a personal list.
